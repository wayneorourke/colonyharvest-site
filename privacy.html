Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

Colony Harvest does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (for example your saved game, upgrades and preferences) locally on your device. This data stays on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Internet use

Colony Harvest works offline and doesn't send data over the internet.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: colonyharvest@gmail.com
