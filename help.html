How to play

Goal

Plant seeds on fertile soil, keep the aliens away while the crops ripen, harvest them, then call a supply ship. The ship buys your crops; you pay for the supplies you order and a delivery fee, and keep the profit.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
Joystick (bottom left)Move your robot.
FIRE (big red button)Shoot aliens. Every shot uses energy, and you can't shoot when energy is very low. Aliens move, so shots can miss; the Targeting Computer upgrade aims ahead of them (your bullets turn orange).
ACT (small button)Use the selected tool on the spot in front of you. Its icon shows which tool is active.
Round buttonsChoose a tool. A greyed-out button means you have run out of that item or don't have enough energy.
🛸 Drone buttonAppears beside the tool buttons once you buy a drone. Tap it to change the drone's job.
PinchZoom in and out.
⚙️ SettingsSave Game, Load Game, Config, Upgrades, Help and Quit.

Tools

ButtonToolNotes
✋PickupCarry rocks, solar panels and fences, then tap ACT again to put them down.
🌱PlantPlants a seed on fertile soil. Tap once to select Plant, then tap again to cycle through the crop types. A message shows each crop's growing time, sell price and trait. Walk up to a ripe crop to harvest it.
⛏️HoeTills poor soil into fertile ground. The button only appears once you have bought a hoe. Each hoe breaks after 10 uses.
☀️SolarRecharges your energy while you stand close.
🧱FenceA wall that slows aliens while they chew through it.
🧨TrapBait that attracts and kills aliens. Costs coins to place.
🔧RepairSpend a spare part to fix a fence, a solar panel or yourself.
🚀ShipCall a supply ship once you have harvested enough crops.

Fertile soil

  • Crops only grow on fertile soil, shown as brown patches on the ground and on the mini map.
  • Each patch is a group of up to 30 cells. There are more patches on Easy than on Normal, and fewest on Hard.
  • On Easy and Normal a green or red box shows the cell your tool will act on: green will work, red won't. There is no box on Hard.
  • Need more land? Buy a hoe and till new cells.
  • Harvesting a damaged crop gives you nothing to sell, so protect crops with fences, traps and your gun.

Aliens

  • Grey: a child. Small and harmless to kill, but it grows up quickly.
  • Green: an adult female. She lays eggs, so deal with her early.
  • Cyan: an adult male. He runs towards the sound of gunfire.

Aliens eat crops, solar panels and fences, and hurt you on contact. Their health shows when they are damaged.

Sense rings

Buy Alien Radar in Upgrades > Gadgets, then switch it on in Settings > Config to see how far aliens can sense things.

  • Purple: traps (the biggest range)
  • Green: ripe crops
  • Yellow: solar panels
  • Brown: fences

If a crop or panel is inside an alien's ring, the alien will head for it. Keep valuable things out of range, or defend them.

Supply ship

  • Harvest enough crops (the counter shows how many you have and how many you need), then tap 🚀.
  • On the order screen, split your cargo points between seeds, solar panels, traps, fences and spare parts. Upgrade Ship Cargo for a bigger budget and a cheaper delivery fee.
  • The ship takes 2 minutes to arrive and drops crates near you if the Supply Beacon is on, otherwise further away.
  • Supply Beacon: a 📡 button charges for 2 minutes, then switches on for 30 seconds. A ship arriving in that window drops crates close to you.
  • The free version allows 3 ship calls per game. Unlock the full game for unlimited calls.

Drone

Buy it in Upgrades > Gadgets. Tap the 🛸 button beside the tools to change its job (for example Follow, or Fetch to collect crates for you).

Saving and loading

  • Save Game (Settings) saves your score, upgrades, crops, seeds and settings. The game also saves when you quit.
  • Load Game (Settings, and on the Game Over screen) returns to your last save. The title screen's LOAD GAME button resumes it.
