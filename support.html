To help us fix a problem, please include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Does the game save my progress?

Yes. It saves automatically on your device when you leave the app.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Colony Harvest works fully offline.

Can I restore progress on a new device?

Progress is stored only on the device you play on, so it doesn't transfer automatically.
