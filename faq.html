Frequently asked questions

How do I change the type of seed I plant?

Tap the 🌱 button once to select Plant, then tap it again to cycle through the crop types.

Why is a button greyed out?

You have run out of that item (seeds, panels, fences, traps, spare parts) or don't have enough energy.

Where is the Hoe button?

It only appears after you buy a hoe in Upgrades > Gadgets.

How do I save my game?

Settings > Save Game. The game also saves when you quit. Use LOAD GAME on the title screen to continue.

What does the free version include?

The free version allows 3 supply-ship calls per game. The one-off in-app unlock removes that limit. If you have bought it before, use Restore Purchases in the game settings.

My crops keep getting eaten

Aliens head for anything inside their sense range. Use fences, traps and your gun, and turn on the alien radar to see the ranges.
